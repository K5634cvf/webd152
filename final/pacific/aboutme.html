Where We Started

Our Founder, Mr. Henry Jones, started Pacific Trails Resort in 1969 as a local buisness with his family. He bought a small plot of land near the Pacific Coast just outside of his hometown of Zephyr, California and built the five "Historic Lodges" which still exist today. Mr. Jones founded Pacific Trails Resort as a peaceful nature getaway to help anyone and everyone rest and relax from their daily lives and enjoy the beautiful nature of the California Coast.

The Origin of our Yurts

During a family vacation to Mongolia Mr. Jones discovered the Yurt: a permanent, tent-like structure used by nomadic people. This inspired Mr. Jones to create Yurts at Pacific Trails Resort and sell Yurts Packages to encourage tourists to stay and rest in our Yurts. Because of our Yurts, Pacific Trails Resort has become a popular tourist destination for people all across the country traveling the Californian Coast.

Our Commitments

Ever since the Jones Family foundedPacific Trails Resort, our goal has always been to provide a Peaceful and Relaxing experience to all of our coustomers by immersing yourself in the beatiful nature of the California Coast.
